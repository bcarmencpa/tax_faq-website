Tax Calculators

Quick estimates for common tax scenarios — for planning purposes only

2025 Federal Tax Bracket Calculator

Estimates your federal income tax based on gross income and filing status. Assumes standard deduction.

* Estimate only. Does not include credits, AMT, NIIT, SE tax, state taxes, or other adjustments.

Self-Employment Tax Calculator

Calculates your Social Security and Medicare taxes as a self-employed individual (Schedule SE).

* 2025 Social Security wage base: $176,100. Does not account for income tax or QBI deduction.

Estimated Tax Payment Calculator

Helps you determine whether you need to make quarterly estimated tax payments and how much.

* Safe harbor: pay 100% of prior year tax (110% if AGI > $150,000) OR 90% of current year tax. Q2 2026 due June 16, 2026.

Withholding Estimator

Estimates how much should be withheld from your paycheck each pay period to cover federal income tax.

* Simplified estimate. Assumes standard deduction only. Use IRS Tax Withholding Estimator (irs.gov/W4app) for a precise result.

Important disclaimer: These calculators provide rough estimates for planning purposes only. They do not account for all tax situations, credits, deductions, alternative minimum tax, state taxes, or recent law changes.
