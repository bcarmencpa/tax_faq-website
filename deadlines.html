Key filing dates for individuals, businesses, and estimated payments — 2026

Tax-Exempt Organization Returns (Form 990)

Annual information return for calendar-year tax-exempt organizations (nonprofits, charities, social clubs). File Form 990, 990-EZ, or 990-N depending on gross receipts. Extension available via Form 8868 (automatic 6-month extension to November 16).

Private Foundation Returns (Form 990-PF)

Annual return for private foundations. Due the 15th day of the 5th month after the end of the organization's tax year. Extension available via Form 8868.

Jun

U.S. Citizens & Resident Aliens Abroad — Automatic Extension

Taxpayers living and working outside the U.S. on April 15 receive an automatic 2-month extension to file Form 1040 (no form required to claim it). Note: this extends the filing deadline only — tax payments were still due April 15 and interest accrues from that date.

Nonresident Alien Return (Form 1040-NR) — No U.S. Withholding

Nonresident aliens with U.S. income not subject to withholding (e.g., business income) must file Form 1040-NR by June 15. Extension available via Form 4868.

Form 941 — Employer's Quarterly Federal Tax Return (Q2 2026)

Employers report wages paid and federal taxes withheld (income tax, Social Security, Medicare) for April–June 2026. Deposit schedule determines whether deposits were already due monthly or semi-weekly. File Form 941 by July 31 regardless.

Form 5500 — Annual Return for Employee Benefit Plans

Annual report for pension, 401(k), profit-sharing, and other employee benefit plans with plan years ending December 31. Extension available via Form 5558 (automatic 2.5-month extension). Plans with fewer than 100 participants may file simplified Form 5500-SF.

PCORI Fee — Form 720 (Applicable Self-Insured Plans)

Sponsors of applicable self-insured health plans and issuers of specified health insurance policies pay the Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute (PCORI) fee. Reported on Form 720, Part II. Fee is $3.22 per covered life for plan years ending in 2024.

Individual Extended Tax Return Due (Form 1040)

Final deadline for individual income tax returns with a valid extension filed by April 15. No further extensions are available after this date. Note: this is the filing deadline — unpaid tax plus interest has been accruing since April 15.

Q4 2026 Estimated Tax Payment Due

Fourth and final quarter 2026 estimated tax payment. Covers September 1 – December 31 income. Alternatively, you can skip this payment if you file your full 2026 tax return and pay any balance due by January 31, 2027.

Jan

W-2s & 1099-NECs Must Be Sent to Recipients

Employers must furnish Form W-2 to employees and payers must send Form 1099-NEC to independent contractors by January 31. W-2s and 1099-NECs are also due to the SSA/IRS by January 31.

Note: When a due date falls on a weekend or federal holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day. Dates shown are for calendar-year filers. Fiscal-year filers have different deadlines based on their year-end. This calendar reflects known 2026 deadlines — confirm with our office for any IRS updates or disaster relief extensions.
