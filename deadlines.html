Tax Deadline Calendar

Key filing dates for individuals, businesses, and estimated payments — October 2026 through June 2027

October 2026

15
Oct

Individual Extended Tax Return Due (Form 1040)

Final deadline for individual income tax returns with a valid extension filed by April 15. No further extensions are available after this date. Note: this is the filing deadline — unpaid tax plus interest has been accruing since April 15.

Individual Extension
4 days
15
Oct

C-Corporation Extended Returns Due (Form 1120)

Extended deadline for calendar-year C corporation income tax returns that filed for a 6-month extension in April.

Business Extension
4 days
15
Oct

FBAR Extended Deadline (FinCEN Form 114)

Automatic extended deadline for the Foreign Bank Account Report (FBAR / FinCEN 114) for U.S. persons with foreign financial accounts exceeding $10,000. Filed electronically at the BSA E-Filing System — not through the IRS.

Individual International Extension
4 days

November 2026

16
Nov

Tax-Exempt Organization Extended Returns Due (Form 990)

Extended deadline for calendar-year tax-exempt organizations that filed Form 8868 in May (November 15 falls on a Sunday, so the deadline moves to Monday, November 16). File Form 990, 990-EZ, or 990-N depending on gross receipts.

Nonprofit Extension
5 weeks

January 2027

15
Jan

Q4 2026 Estimated Tax Payment Due

Fourth and final quarter 2026 estimated tax payment. Covers September 1 – December 31 income. Pay online at IRS Direct Pay or with Form 1040-ES voucher.

Individual Estimated Tax
3 months
31
Jan

W-2s & 1099-NECs Must Be Sent to Recipients

Employers must furnish Form W-2 to employees and payers must send Form 1099-NEC to independent contractors by January 31. W-2s and 1099-NECs are also due to the SSA/IRS by January 31.

Business Payroll
3 months

March 2027

15
Mar

Partnership & S-Corporation Returns Due (Form 1065 / 1120-S)

Deadline for 2026 partnership (Form 1065) and S corporation (Form 1120-S) income tax returns, or to file Form 7004 for an automatic 6-month extension. S corporations must provide K-1s to shareholders by this date.

Business
5 months

April 2027

15
Apr

2026 Individual Tax Returns Due (Form 1040)

Deadline to file 2026 individual income tax returns or file Form 4868 for an automatic 6-month extension. Any balance due must be paid by April 15 even if you extend the filing. Also the deadline to make 2026 IRA and Roth IRA contributions.

Individual
6 months
15
Apr

Q1 2027 Estimated Tax Payment Due

First quarter 2027 estimated tax payment for individuals, sole proprietors, partners, and S corporation shareholders. Covers January 1 – February 28 income. Pay online at IRS Direct Pay or with Form 1040-ES.

Individual Estimated Tax
6 months
15
Apr

C-Corporation & Trust/Estate Returns Due (Form 1120 / 1041)

Deadline for 2026 calendar-year C corporation income tax returns (Form 1120) and trust & estate income tax returns (Form 1041), or to file for an extension.

Business Individual
6 months

June 2027

15
Jun

Q2 2027 Estimated Tax Payment Due

Second quarter 2027 estimated tax payment. Covers March 1 – April 30 income. Pay online at IRS Direct Pay or with Form 1040-ES voucher.

Individual Estimated Tax
8 months

Note: When a due date falls on a weekend or federal holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day. Dates shown are for calendar-year filers. Fiscal-year filers have different deadlines based on their year-end. Calendar last updated October 2026 — confirm with our office for any IRS updates or disaster relief extensions.
